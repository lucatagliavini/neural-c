## Open Considerations

- A parent-directory `fsync` error after `rename` or `unlink` is necessarily
  ambiguous: the operation reports failure although the new checkpoint or
  removal may already be visible. All visible checkpoint outcomes remain valid,
  and 5.2 reconciles the possible weights-plus-checkpoint state on the next
  resume.
- Periodic checkpoint serialization is synchronous at epoch boundaries. This
  is intentional for exact recoverability but can add latency for large models;
  no asynchronous snapshot design is currently planned.
- Advisory `flock` requires the project filesystem to provide Linux-compatible
  shared/exclusive locking. The supported local Linux targets do; unusual or
  misconfigured network filesystems remain an operational consideration.
- Graceful signals are intentionally honored only at coherent epoch
  boundaries. Shutdown latency is therefore bounded by the remaining work in
  the current epoch plus synchronous checkpoint persistence, not by an
  immediate cancellation of active worker tasks.
