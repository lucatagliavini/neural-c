## Open Considerations

- Native ppc64le remains a distinct future qualification opportunity. QEMU
  cannot establish real PowerPC performance, kernel integration, sanitizer
  runtime behavior, or hardware-specific race behavior.
- Cross-architecture prediction comparison deliberately permits absolute
  `1e-14` or relative `1e-12` differences because target `libm`
  implementations may differ in their last bits. Protocols, digests, PRNG
  integers, persistence round trips, and within-runtime worker comparisons
  remain exact.
- The cross-runtime scripts assume invocation from the repository root, like
  the existing Make and test commands.
