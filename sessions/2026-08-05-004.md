## Objective and update contract

- The objective is mean data loss plus `l1 * sum(abs(theta))` plus
  `(l2 / 2) * sum(theta * theta)` over all weights and optional biases.
- L1 uses subgradient `-1`, `0`, or `1`, including zero at an exactly zero
  parameter. L2 contributes `l2 * theta` to the gradient.
- Penalty accumulation is Neumaier-compensated. Products, sums, and gradient
  changes are checked before mutation; regularization gradient addition is
  transactional.
- The coordinator update order is ordered mean data gradient, regularization,
  norm measurement, clipping, future optimizer transformation, and model
  update. Workers never add parameter-derived terms.
- Reported `loss` remains coherent post-epoch data loss. `objective` adds the
  same model's penalty. Validation/test evaluation and early stopping continue
  to use data loss only.

## Reporting and continuation
